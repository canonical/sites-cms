# Signing in

## Today

The CMS is not yet deployed for editors. Developers running it on their own
machine sign in at http://localhost:8000/admin/ with the username `admin`
and the password `admin`.

On a test deployment, before company sign-in is switched on, an
administrator creates your account and gives you a random password. Change
it after your first sign-in: open Account, then the Password section.

## When it is deployed

You will sign in with your Canonical account, the same one you use for
other company tools. There is no separate password for the CMS.

Anyone with a company account can sign in. A new account can look around
but cannot change anything until an administrator adds it to a group:

| Group | Can do |
|---|---|
| Writer | Write posts and send them for review |
| Editor | Review, publish and unpublish posts |
| Admin | Everything, including managing who is in which group |

If you sign in and see a page saying you do not have access yet, ask the
web team to add you to a group.

This page will be updated with the real address and screenshots when
company sign-in is switched on.
